Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about emergency dental care and our 24 hour dentist service.

Is there a 24 hour dentist near me in Washington, DC?

Yes. Emergency Dental Service is a 24 hour dentist option for the Washington, DC area. Call 202-970-9589 any time you have severe pain, swelling or a dental injury and we’ll arrange care as quickly as possible.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, a knocked-out or broken tooth, a swollen face or jaw, a dental abscess, uncontrolled bleeding and mouth injuries are all emergencies. If you aren’t sure, call us and we’ll help you decide.

Can I get a same day dental appointment?

In most urgent cases, yes. We prioritize emergency patients so you can be seen the same day for toothache relief, tooth extraction, root canal treatment or tooth repair.

What should I do while I wait to be seen?

Rinse gently with warm water, use a cold compress on your cheek, and take an over-the-counter pain reliever as directed on the label. If a tooth was knocked out, hold it by the crown, keep it moist in milk, and come in right away. Don’t place aspirin directly on the gum.

How much does emergency dental care cost?

The cost depends on the treatment you need. We explain your options and costs before we begin so there are no surprises. Call us to ask about payment and insurance.
